## Text

Before trusting a guard/gate/save-path's verdict: check the mechanism's own correctness, its inputs' currency, and re-confirm claimed effects actually persisted. A false pass is worse than a failure.

A green gate shows its subject ran and its input resolved; a check that reads the subject's own
output cannot detect the subject's absence (Vantage: verify the step ran; TerrainDiffusion R26,
R37, R38, R39).

A check is proved on a case whose answer is already known — a positive control run through the same
harness — before its verdict on an unknown case counts; a null result without that control may be a
dead instrument (Vantage: positive control; bfm-decomp R86 and the known-true case).
