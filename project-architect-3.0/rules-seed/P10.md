## Text

Rules accumulate through one channel, and the developer still ratifies.

An expert that meets a correction, a repeated mistake or a gotcha worth a norm writes one
`Rule candidate: <text>` line in `tasks/T<n>.md`. `phaseend_index.py` collects every such line
into the PhaseEnd's `## Rules proposed`. The next planner session puts them to the developer one
at a time and runs `rules_add.py add|supersede|retire`, which writes `rules/<id>.md` and its
`rules/INDEX.md` line. A superseded rule stays listed, marked; nothing is deleted.

Not everything that hurt is a rule. A technique goes to the cookbook (`cookbook_add.sh`); a
repeatable procedure goes to a skill (`skill_add.py`); a one-off is a deviation recorded in the
task summary and nothing more. Only a headline short enough to live in the `project-architect` skill is
promoted into it; the full text always stays in `rules/<id>.md`.
