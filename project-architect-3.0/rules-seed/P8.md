## Text

A phase closes in scripts, with two authored blocks.

The closer expert (a PHASE-END brief) verifies the milestone, promotes `generalizable:` gotchas to
the cookbook and `workflow:` ones to skills, appends the surviving binding decisions to
`HOW_WE_WORK.md ## Standing decisions`, and authors exactly two things: the binding-decisions
block and the plain-English recap.

`phaseend_index.py assemble <N>` builds everything else in `PhaseEnd_Phase<N>.md` from
`tasks/*.md`, the plan and the indexes. `phaseend_index.py lint` fails while any placeholder
remains; `commit_task.sh phase-end` commits it; `phaseend_index.py archive` moves `current/` to
`phase-<N>/` with `git mv` and opens a fresh `current/`. A closed generation adds
`genend_index.py assemble <G>`.

A `review: yes` task closes on the developer's word, the phase does not close while one is open,
and the closer asks "anything else before I close?" first.

Then the turn ends. The next phase is a new session started by `launch.py`: never a continuation
of this one, never a preview of what comes next.
