## Text

A phase closes in scripts, with no agent.

After the developer's yes at the close question, Claude calls `release({hold: "close", note:
"<its phase report>"})`. The mod writes the note to `phase-ends/current/RECAP.md` as `## Recap`
(plus `## Generation Recap` at a generation end) and runs the close: `phaseend_index.py assemble
<N>` builds `PhaseEnd_Phase<N>.md` from the recap, `tasks/*.md`, the plan and the indexes;
`commit_task.sh phase-end` commits it; `phaseend_index.py archive` moves `current/` to
`phase-<N>/` with `git mv` and opens a fresh `current/`. A closed generation adds
`genend_index.py assemble <G>`.

A `review: yes` task closes on the developer's word, the phase does not close while one is open,
and "anything else before I close?" is asked first (by Claude).

Then the next phase starts from its own approved plan, a fresh expert
per task, nothing carried over from this phase's tasks.
