## Text

Two gates per phase, both developer-visible, neither of them mid-task.

Gate 1, the plan: Claude drafts it with the `pa-planning` skill, exploring through retrievers,
into `.run/`, and the mod presents it in the plan-mode view. The developer approves. `plan_edit.py approve` stamps `Approved`, `Planner` and `Plan-hash`, and everything
above `## Tasks` is immutable from that moment.

Gate 2, the milestone: the developer confirms it at the close question. Their yes closes and
archives the phase (P8).

A task the plan marks `review: yes` pauses for the developer's decisions and closes on their
word; "anything else before I close?" is asked once before the close (by Claude).

Between the gates nobody asks (P5). Neither gate carries an effort or mode ceremony: every agent
file pins its own model and effort, and Claude follows the developer's `/model` and `/effort`.
