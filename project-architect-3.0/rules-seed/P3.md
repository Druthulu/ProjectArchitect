## Text

Two gates per phase, both developer-visible, neither of them mid-task.

Gate 1, the plan: a planner session (`launch.py` picks `planner-phase`) explores through
retrievers, drafts into `.run/`, and presents it through the question picker. The developer
approves. `plan_edit.py approve` stamps `Approved`, `Planner` and `Plan-hash`, and everything
above `## Tasks` is immutable from that moment.

Gate 2, the milestone: the closer expert (a PHASE-END brief) runs `phaseend_index.py verify`,
which executes every `verified by:` clause of the milestone and prints GREEN or RED per clause,
recorded in the PhaseEnd `VERIFIED:` line. Green closes and archives the phase; the developer
sees the recap afterward.

A task the plan marks `review: yes` pauses for the developer's decisions and closes on their
word; the closer asks "anything else before I close?" before verify and archive.

Between the gates nobody asks (P5). Neither gate carries an effort or mode ceremony: `launch.py`
sets the session model, effort and permission mode, and every agent file pins its own.
